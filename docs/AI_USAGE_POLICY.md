# AI/Agent Usage Policy

**Last updated:** 2025

> **Status:** Draft. Sections marked `[REQUIRES COUNSEL]` require review by qualified legal counsel before publication.

This AI/Agent Usage Policy governs the use of AI-powered features on the Lazynext platform, including content generation, AI agents, and automated workflows. AI generation is powered by Atlas Cloud and other provider models. By using AI features you agree to this policy. `[REQUIRES COUNSEL]`

---

## 1. Acceptable AI Use Cases

- Generating marketing copy, ad scripts, and creative briefs for your brand.
- Producing storyboards, hooks, and content variants for A/B testing.
- Automating repetitive creative workflows within your authorized workspace.
- Using AI agents to manage tasks, schedules, and internal data you own.
- Analyzing performance data and generating insights for campaign optimization.

## 2. Prohibited AI Use Cases

- Generating deepfakes or synthetic media that impersonate real people without consent.
- Creating misinformation, disinformation, or content designed to deceive the public.
- Harassment, bullying, or content that targets individuals or protected groups.
- Generating illegal content, including content that promotes illegal activities.
- Producing content that infringes on intellectual property rights.
- Using AI to circumvent safety controls, access controls, or rate limits.
- Deploying AI agents that operate without appropriate human oversight for high-risk decisions.

## 3. User Responsibility for AI-Generated Content

You are solely responsible for the prompts you submit, the content generated by AI features, and the actions taken by AI agents you create. You must review AI-generated content before publishing or distributing it. AI-generated content may contain inaccuracies, biases, or unintended outputs. `[REQUIRES COUNSEL]`

## 4. AI Agent Constraints

AI agents and automations you create operate under your account and workspace credentials. You are responsible for configuring appropriate instructions, tool access, and scope limitations. Agents that cause harm through unauthorized actions are your responsibility. We recommend human-in-the-loop review for automations that affect production systems, financial transactions, or external communications. `[REQUIRES COUNSEL]`

## 5. Data Used for AI Processing

When you use AI features, your inputs (prompts, images, documents) are sent to AI providers (Atlas Cloud and others) for processing. These providers may retain data temporarily for safety and quality purposes. We do not use your private workspace content to train AI models. See our Privacy Policy and subprocessor list for details. `[REQUIRES COUNSEL]`

## 6. Model Provider Disclosures

AI generation on Lazynext is powered by Atlas Cloud and other providers. Your use of AI features is also subject to the usage policies of these providers. We may restrict or modify AI features based on provider policy changes. Provider-specific terms apply to outputs generated using their models. `[REQUIRES COUNSEL]`

## 7. Human Oversight Requirements

- Review AI-generated content before publishing or sending to external audiences.
- Maintain human-in-the-loop approval for automations affecting production systems.
- Verify AI-generated claims, statistics, and facts before public distribution.
- Monitor AI agent actions and intervene when outputs deviate from intent.

## 8. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of AI-generated content or agent behavior that violates this policy, report it promptly to [ai-policy@lazynext.com](mailto:ai-policy@lazynext.com). We investigate reports and may suspend access for users who violate this policy. `[REQUIRES COUNSEL]`

## 9. Changes to Policy

We may update this AI/Agent Usage Policy as AI technology and regulations evolve. Continued use of AI features after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. `[REQUIRES COUNSEL]`
